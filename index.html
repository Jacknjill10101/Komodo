Questions people actually ask

Is it actually as good as cloud AI?

Different trade-off: frontier cloud models are smarter; on-device models are private and always available. Komodo's catalog is curated so that what runs well on your specific iPhone is what you see first — and Pro lets power users import any model and quantization they want.

Why does downloading need Wi-Fi?

The MLX framework it builds on doesn't support cellular downloads — a framework limitation, not the app's. Once a model is on your phone, chat runs on cellular or fully offline.

Which iPhones are supported?

Models are sized to each chip and memory class, with warnings before risky ones. Apple Intelligence support needs iPhone 15 Pro or later. Komodo backs off gracefully instead of crashing — it knows what your phone can handle.

Why one-time purchase, not a subscription?

Because the inference is free — it runs on your hardware. There is no GPU farm to subsidize, so a subscription would be a tax on your own phone. $3.99 once, Family Sharing included.
